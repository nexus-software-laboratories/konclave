UNSIGNED KONCLAVE PRERELEASE

This artifact does not carry a platform code signature or notarization.
Do not treat its origin as verified by the operating system.

Konclave publishes unsigned prereleases so code-signing enrollment does not block
the open self-hosted MVP. Integrity manifests, software bills of materials, and
build provenance are separate release metadata and do not replace a code signature.

SHA-256 verification detects changed or incomplete files after obtaining a release
set. It does not prove who created that release set. Review the recorded source
revision and obtain artifacts through a channel you trust.
